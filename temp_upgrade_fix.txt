Alternative approach to avoid India export issues:

Instead of:
1. Create invoice for proration
2. Update subscription with price_data

Do this:
1. Calculate prorated credit
2. Create ONE-TIME invoice for the upgrade with the EXACT prorated amount
3. Cancel old subscription (at period end)
4. Create NEW subscription starting immediately at new price

This avoids Stripe's export transaction rules because we're creating a fresh subscription, not modifying an existing one.
